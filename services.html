EXPERTISE / A PROPOSED SERVICE OVERVIEW

A starting point.
Not a one-size-fits-all plan.

Aplica’s published services span implementation, assessment, migration, integration and support. This proposed structure groups them around why a customer might need help.

Discuss your situation ↗

01 / IMPLEMENT

Build the right foundation.

For a new implementation or a business moving beyond disconnected tools.

What needs to be understood?

  • Current processes, systems and business priorities
  • Data sources, interfaces and responsibilities
  • User roles, adoption needs and constraints

A possible engagement

  • Business process discovery and scope definition
  • Platform fit and implementation planning
  • Configuration, integration, testing and user preparation

Illustrative engagement structure. Availability, scope, timing and support terms must be agreed with Aplica. No fixed delivery or service-level promise is made.

Talk through your challenge ↗

02 / IMPROVE

Get a clearer view of what needs to change.

For an existing Dynamics environment, a migration decision or an implementation that needs attention.

Useful questions to bring

  • Which product and version are you using?
  • What is failing, slow or difficult for users?
  • Who owns the current implementation and its dependencies?

A possible engagement

  • System review and recommendations
  • Upgrade or migration assessment
  • Project recovery scoping and prioritised remediation

Illustrative engagement structure. Availability, scope, timing and support terms must be agreed with Aplica. No fixed delivery or service-level promise is made.

Talk through your challenge ↗

03 / SUPPORT

Plan beyond the go-live date.

For teams seeking application support, training and continued improvement.

What needs to be established?

  • Products, customisations and integrations in scope
  • Support ownership and existing agreements
  • User knowledge gaps and improvement priorities

A possible engagement

  • Support transition and service scope
  • Product training and knowledge transfer
  • An agreed improvement backlog

Illustrative engagement structure. Availability, scope, timing and support terms must be agreed with Aplica. No fixed delivery or service-level promise is made.

PLATFORM FIT

Choose for the business.
Not the label.

Aplica’s public offer includes Business Central, Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and Power BI, alongside other applications. Confirm current product coverage and assess the requirements before selecting a platform.

Microsoft describes Business Central as ERP for small and midsize businesses. That is a product positioning statement—not a substitute for assessing your business complexity.
