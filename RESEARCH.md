# Aplica: desk research & strategic hypotheses
## A decision brief before the next design round
**Research date:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** Working recommendations; not approved positioning or validated customer research.

## 1. Executive recommendation
Do not commission another visual redesign yet. First validate a more specific proposition: **practical Microsoft Dynamics implementation, improvement and ongoing support for selected Middle East business needs.** Investigate whether rental/project operations, trade/distribution, and regional extensions provide credible areas of specialism.

Retain the current blue concept as an exploration of clarity—not evidence that the palette, headline, page hierarchy or service priorities are correct. Its “connected systems” message explains the category but does not yet explain why Aplica should be selected.

A potential differentiation territory is **practical delivery and collaborative problem-solving**, supported by specific customer stories. This is a hypothesis, not a proven advantage over competitors.

## 2. Method and limitations
Reviewed the supplied meeting transcript; extracted public Aplica homepage and product content; sampled public messaging from Intwo, Alletec, Levtech and Dynamics Center; checked a Microsoft-published customer story and Microsoft's Business Central product page.

This is a purposive, small website sample—not a market census, competitor ranking, independent service-quality assessment, technical SEO audit, visual usability study or customer study. Competitor claims describe what those companies publish, not independently verified results. No analytics, CRM, Search Console, commercial results, project records or customer interviews were available. No search-volume, share-of-market, pricing or conversion conclusions are justified. Text extraction does not establish actual on-screen prominence or visual quality. Retrieval may be affected by changing pages, caching and access conditions.

## 3. What the evidence actually shows
### Aplica already has proof material
The public homepage contains named testimonial excerpts associated with Seven Tides, IIHS, Albaddad and AWJ Holding, plus customer logos. Excerpts describe ERP selection, collaboration, rental-process delivery and ongoing support. These are company-published statements, not independent verification. Confirm attribution, current permissions, full context and project scope before reusing them. [A1]

**Correction to the earlier interpretation:** the evidence gap is not a total absence of social proof. It is insufficiently developed, accessible and validated project evidence. Avoid commissioning interviews as if nothing exists.

The homepage's testimonial links pointed to `/client-speak/`; that destination returned a page-not-found message during this review. This is an observed destination failure, not proof of a Google penalty. [A1, A2]

### The offer is broader and more specific than the latest preview
The homepage lists Business Central, Finance & Operations, customer engagement, Power BI, legacy AX/NAV, GCC payroll, industry offerings, implementation, upgrades, recovery, integration, training and support. Industry entries include rental, construction, real estate, trade/distribution and warehouse management. [A1]

The business-applications page describes PayApp D365 as an Aplica payroll add-on associated with Finance/Operations and Business Central. Product availability, ownership, supported versions, jurisdictions and current capability remain unverified. [A3]

**Implication:** replacing all of this with three generic business-function cards risks hiding useful selection criteria. Prioritise rather than erase the actual offer.

### “Connected systems” is category messaging
Aplica's Dynamics overview discusses breaking down ERP/CRM silos; Levtech's Business Central page discusses connecting the business; Microsoft's product page discusses reducing data silos, connecting applications and business management. [A4, C3, M1]

**Implication:** connection is a useful benefit, not a defensible USP on current evidence.

## 5. Buyer hypotheses—not established personas
| Candidate buying situation | Possible participants | Information they may need | What could disprove it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growing business outgrowing separate accounting/operational tools | Finance lead, owner/GM, IT evaluator | Fit, migration, business disruption, scope and operating cost | Most profitable enquiries are already-enterprise Dynamics projects |
| Existing Dynamics customer with delivery or support problems | IT/ERP manager, operations sponsor, procurement | Diagnostic approach, transition responsibilities, continuity, relevant experience | Recovery/support is low priority, lacks capacity or produces poor-fit enquiries |
| Rental, project or distribution operation needing specific workflows | Operations lead, finance, ERP manager | Workflow detail, integrations, reporting and actual industry examples | Public industry offerings are legacy content or few projects can be substantiated |

Company size, budget, country priority and decision-making roles need validation. Do not invent employee or revenue bands to create an appearance of precision.

## 6. Prioritised hypothesis register
**Confidence refers to support for the hypothesis, not certainty of commercial success.**

| ID | Hypothesis | Basis / confidence | Test | Design consequence if supported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Evidence presentation is a more immediate credibility opportunity than adding more generic claims. | Existing excerpts, inaccessible detail destination, competitor references. Moderate. | Inventory approved proof; review whether target readers can find relevant evidence. | Place a specific customer story early; repair evidence journeys. |
| H2 | Practical, collaborative delivery is a promising positioning territory. | Aplica-published customer excerpts. Low–moderate; selection bias. | Review won/lost proposals and ask customers why Aplica was chosen and retained. | Explain responsibilities, decisions, adoption and support—not just tools. |
| H3 | Selected industry workflows can be more distinctive than broad digital-transformation language. | Public vertical pages and rental testimonial. Moderate capability signal; low priority certainty. | Ask delivery/sales for project count, repeatability, profitability and references by vertical. | Build only the strongest 2–3 industry journeys first. |
| H4 | Existing-system improvement deserves a route alongside new implementation. | Published recovery, migration and support services. Moderate availability signal; demand unknown. | Classify recent qualified enquiries and delivery capacity. | Offer “Implement”, “Upgrade/improve” and “Support” routes. |
| H5 | Regional capability should be demonstrated, not implied by a UAE headline. | Meeting focus and payroll/localisation content. Moderate. | Confirm country-specific delivery examples and actual scope with technical leads. | Use concrete workflows and references; no blanket compliance claims. |
| H6 | A light blue-led presentation can suit the intended accessible consultancy character. | Client discussed blue; current concept exploration. Low until approved. | Review representative screens against brand goals and comprehension tasks. | Develop palette/system after approval; do not justify with colour psychology alone. |
| H7 | Authentic people and project imagery can improve perceived specificity. | Need to explain who delivers and how. Low; design hypothesis. | Compare actual assets and ask readers what the images help them understand. | Combine real photography, diagrams and approved screenshots. |

## 7. Positioning options to validate
### Recommended working territory: practical delivery
“Microsoft Dynamics expertise for the way your business actually works.”
Support with specific implementation, improvement and support stories. This is proposed copy, not an established competitive advantage. Avoid claiming all projects or industries are covered.

### Alternative: industry-specific expertise
“Microsoft business applications for complex rental, project and distribution operations.”
Potentially more specific, but use only if the team confirms commercial priority, repeatable capability and publishable projects. Do not select these industries just because pages exist.

### Keep as supporting message: connected business
“Bring finance, operations and customer information together.”
Clear benefit, but insufficient differentiation on its own.

## 8. Implications for the existing designs
**First concept:** keep the ambition for technical depth and distinctive presentation. Do not retain density, unsupported case narratives or delivery promises merely for impact.

**Second concept:** keep breathing room, simpler language and restrained blue exploration. Improve specificity. “Stories in development” and a large photo placeholder are internal review devices, not production trust content. Avoid placing unapproved process descriptions in front of real evidence.

**Next direction:** business clarity + technical specificity + authentic people + approved proof. Colour is secondary to positioning. No research here proves a light site will convert better than a dark one.

## 9. Proposed information architecture
Working navigation: **Services | Platforms | Industries | Client stories | About | Contact**. Reduce or combine categories if approved content cannot support them; test labels before finalising.

Services: implementation; upgrades/migration; system review/improvement; integration; support/training. Confirm priority and capacity.
Platforms: Business Central; Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management; customer engagement; Power BI/Power Platform only where supported. Validate current Microsoft naming and Aplica's actual coverage with the technical team.
Industries: publish only evidence-backed priorities. Retain legacy page relevance through an SEO migration plan rather than deleting URLs blindly.

Proposed homepage sequence:
1. Specific proposition and recognisable Microsoft platform reference.
2. Approved, attributable customer proof.
3. “What brings you here?” implementation/improvement/support routes.
4. Priority capability/platform fit.
5. One substantive customer story.
6. Selected industry experience.
7. Delivery approach and real team.
8. Useful FAQs and an appropriate contact action.

This is a recommendation to prototype, not an SEO keyword map or guaranteed conversion sequence.

## 10. Lightweight validation under constraints
### Minimum internal session: 60–90 minutes
Ask leadership, sales and delivery to bring three good-fit projects, one lost opportunity and one difficult engagement. Establish priority services, best-fit customers, actual reasons for selection, capacity and publishable proof. Record disagreement rather than averaging it away.

### Minimum evidence collection
- Existing full testimonial texts and permissions.
- Two potential case studies with customer, problem, scope, decisions, evidence and limitations.
- Recent enquiry/proposal sample, appropriately redacted and shared with permission.
- Current capability/product ownership list.
- Available authentic photography and screenshot permissions.

### Short prototype tasks
Ask a few relevant readers to explain the offer, find a service for an actual scenario, locate credible evidence and identify how to contact Aplica. Small qualitative sessions reveal issues; they do not produce statistically reliable conversion rates.

### Decision gate before full build
Approve one priority buyer situation, one positioning statement, priority offerings, proof inventory, sitemap and one homepage/service-page direction. Keep unresolved assumptions visible.

## 11. What to propose to the client
“Given limited access to customer and commercial data, we have completed an initial desk-research review. It suggests that the opportunity is not simply a newer-looking website: it is clearer service selection and stronger presentation of Aplica's actual delivery experience. We propose validating the priority customer situations and available evidence in a focused workshop, then refining the design around those findings.”

Do not promise ranking, lead volume, AI citations or conversion uplift from this research. Agree measurement after baseline data and tracking access are available.
