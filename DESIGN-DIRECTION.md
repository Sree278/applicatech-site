# Human understanding / Concept 06
31 October 2026

The user selected **Human-led business** over the editorial and sculptural alternatives.

## What changes, rather than just more motion
1. **Human-first opening:** an editorial split layout pairs direct Dynamics messaging with large collaborative-workplace photography. The decorative WebGL hero and CGI gallery are removed. No scroll-pinned opening, headline fade or scroll hijacking.
2. **Warmer colour architecture:** warm ivory #F5F2EB, deep ink-blue typography #182E47, cobalt actions #1B43A5 and restrained copper labels. Dark blue chapters provide contrast between the lighter people-led sections. Blue remains the core brand signal; this is an exploratory direction rather than a completed identity.
3. **Photography with clear limits:** stock imagery illustrates collaboration and discussion. Captions explicitly state that these are not Aplica personnel or customer projects. Approved real regional team/workplace/customer images should replace stock before a full production launch. See IMAGE-CREDITS.md.
4. **Editorial hierarchy:** large paired sans/serif headings, varied image ratios, concise labels, asymmetric desktop compositions and mobile-specific crops. The typography and composition do the expressive work; animation is not the main attraction.
5. **Human-led delivery:** a full photographic panel anchors the approach section instead of decorative orbital geometry. The linear Understand / Define / Deliver / Improve story stays readable without animation.
6. **Subordinate technology:** the four-scenario explorer remains an optional explanatory aid. Its pause control is moved next to the explorer, rather than occupying the homepage header. Information is still real HTML and the illustration has a non-WebGL fallback.
7. **Consistent detail pages:** implementation and customer-story format use the same visual language, distinct photographs and a slightly different surface rhythm.
8. **Clean finish:** each page ends at its footer; no design-review dialog or trailing content.

## Interaction and content
Keyboard tabs, menu focus return, expand/collapse service rows, detail-page navigation and validated email-draft enquiries are retained. Reduced-motion support remains. No new animation is added for this direction.

The customer-story page remains a template. No customer identity, measurable outcome, quote, credential or service guarantee has been invented. Existing scope and commercial disclaimers remain; their final wording needs Aplica approval.

## Verification boundaries
Engineering checks do not establish customer preference, conversion uplift, award quality or full accessibility conformance. Real target-customer testing, assistive-technology/browser/device coverage, photography permissions, commercial approval and production backend/legal/security work remain outstanding.
