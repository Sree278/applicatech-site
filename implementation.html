01 / BUYING SITUATION

Planning a new system?
Start with the business.

This may be the right discussion if you are moving beyond disconnected tools, evaluating a new ERP, or bringing teams onto a shared platform. It is not a recommendation to replace your current systems without assessment.

BUSINESS SPONSOR

Define what better means.

03 / A POSSIBLE ENGAGEMENT

Agree the outputs.
Not just the activities.

The following is an illustrative way to structure discussions with Aplica. Actual outputs, responsibilities and timing must be agreed.

  1. Discovery: business priorities, process understanding and a documented requirements baseline.
  2. Planning: platform-fit discussion, scope, dependencies, responsibilities and delivery approach.
  3. Implementation: configuration, integration and data preparation within the agreed scope.
  4. Validation: realistic business scenarios, user preparation and agreed acceptance.
  5. Transition: go-live readiness, ownership, support and a plan for continued improvement.

No fixed completion time, budget, service level or business result is promised by this template.

04 / WHAT TO ASK FOR

A relevant story.
With the detail intact.

Ask for an example that matches your business situation. Useful evidence explains the customer's starting point, Aplica's actual scope, key decisions and an outcome the customer can substantiate.

05 / BEFORE WE TALK

Useful questions.

How is the platform selected?

Establish requirements, complexity, integration needs, current tools and constraints. Product fit needs a technical and commercial assessment rather than a decision based on a website diagram.

How long will an implementation take?

Timing depends on scope, data readiness, integrations, user availability and governance. Discuss these dependencies before accepting a schedule.

Can we start if we do not have a complete specification?

A description of the business, current landscape, main challenges and intended outcomes can start the conversation. Discovery can clarify what still needs to be defined.

What happens after go-live?

Agree support ownership, training and improvement responsibilities as part of the engagement. This prototype does not imply a particular support agreement.
