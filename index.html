06 / BEFORE WE TALK

A useful place
to begin.

Do we need a new system?＋

Not necessarily. Start with the business problem and current landscape. An assessment can help distinguish process changes, system improvements and replacement needs.

Can you help with an existing Dynamics environment?＋

Aplica publicly lists audit, migration, project recovery and support services. Discuss your product version, current issues and responsibilities to establish whether the team is a fit.

What should we bring to a first conversation?＋

A description of your business, existing systems, main challenges and desired changes. You do not need a completed technical specification.

Can we see a relevant customer example?＋

Ask for a reference relevant to your situation. Any project information or customer introduction should be shared subject to permission and confidentiality.
