06 / BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful
starting points.

Do we need to replace our current systems?+

Not necessarily. Start by mapping the business problems and existing landscape. A review can help distinguish process changes, improvements to current tools and genuine replacement needs.

What should we prepare for an initial discussion?+

A short description of your business, current systems, main challenges and desired outcomes is a good start. You do not need a finished technical specification.

How are scope and timing established?+

They should follow an assessment of requirements, integrations, data readiness, user needs and dependencies. This preview intentionally makes no fixed implementation-time promise.

Can we see relevant customer examples?+

Ask about examples relevant to your situation. Published stories and references should only be shared once the evidence and customer permissions are confirmed.
